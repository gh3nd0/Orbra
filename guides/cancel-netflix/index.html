How to cancel Netflix in the UK

Last updated: [fill in when checked]

Quick answer

Go to netflix.com/cancelplan, sign in, click Cancel, then Finish Cancellation. You can keep watching until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Cancel on the Netflix website

  1. Go to netflix.com/cancelplan in a web browser and sign in.
  2. Click Cancel.
  3. Click Finish Cancellation.

Netflix sends a confirmation email to the address on your account. Keep it until your access ends.

Deleting the Netflix app or signing out doesn’t cancel your membership. You’ll keep being charged until you cancel.

If there’s no Cancel button

You probably pay for Netflix through another company, such as Apple, your broadband or TV provider, or your mobile network. Open the Membership section of your Netflix Account page. It will either show you how to cancel or tell you which company to contact.

If you pay through Apple, follow our guide to cancelling a subscription on iPhone or iPad.

What happens after you cancel

  • You can keep watching until the end of your current billing period. You won’t be charged again.
  • If your account is on hold when you cancel, it closes straight away.
  • If you restart later, Netflix keeps your viewing activity, ratings and recommendations for 24 months after your account closes.

Questions

Will I lose access straight away?

No. You can watch until the end of the billing period you’ve already paid for, unless your account was on hold, in which case it closes immediately.

Does deleting the app cancel Netflix?

No. Deleting the app or signing out doesn’t cancel your membership. You need to cancel on your Account page.

Can I get my profiles and history back if I rejoin?

Netflix keeps your viewing activity, ratings and recommendations for 24 months after your account closes, so they’ll be there if you restart within that time.
