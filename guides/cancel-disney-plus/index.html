How to cancel Disney+ in the UK

Last updated: [fill in when checked]

Quick answer

Sign in to Disney+ in a web browser, select your profile, then Account, choose your subscription and select Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of your billing period or minimum term.

Cancel in a web browser

Disney+ says to cancel in a web browser on a computer or phone.

  1. Sign in to your Disney+ account in a web browser.
  2. Select your profile, then Account.
  3. Select your Disney+ subscription.
  4. Select Cancel Subscription. You can skip the short survey about why you’re leaving.

When your access ends

You can keep watching until the end of your current billing period. If you agreed to a minimum term that runs longer, you’ll keep access, and keep paying, until that term ends.

Cancelling doesn’t delete your Disney+ account. If you change your mind before the end of your billing period, you can restart from the Account page without losing anything.

Pause instead of cancelling

If you pay Disney+ monthly and are billed by Disney+ directly, you can pause instead. Go to Account, select your subscription, then Pause Subscription, and choose how long. You won’t be charged while it’s paused. Some promotions and minimum-term plans can’t be paused.

Paying through Sky or another partner

If Disney+ comes through another company, such as Sky, a mobile network or Apple, you need to cancel with that company. For Apple, see how to cancel a subscription on iPhone or iPad.

Questions

Can I cancel Disney+ on my TV?

Disney+ says to cancel by signing in through a web browser on a computer or phone, then going to Account.

Do I lose access as soon as I cancel?

No. You keep access until the end of your billing period, or until the end of your minimum term if that’s later.

Can I pause Disney+ instead?

Yes, if you pay monthly and are billed by Disney+ directly. Some promotional and minimum-term plans can’t be paused.
