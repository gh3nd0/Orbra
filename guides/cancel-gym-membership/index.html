How to cancel a gym membership early

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quick answer

Check your contract for the minimum term and notice period. You’ll usually have to pay for the rest of the minimum term, but a gym should normally let you leave early if a serious illness, injury or a big drop in income means you can’t carry on.

This guide covers England. Rules are similar elsewhere in the UK, but check Citizens Advice Scotland or Advice NI for local details.

Start with your contract

Look for three things: the minimum term, the notice period, and what it costs to leave early. Check your contract; many gyms ask for 30 days or one month’s notice. If you cancel a one-year contract after six months, you’ll usually have to pay for the remaining six.

Simply changing your mind usually isn’t enough to get out of a contract early.

When a gym should let you leave early

  • Serious illness or injury that stops you exercising. You’ll need evidence from a doctor or other medical professional.
  • A big change in money, such as losing your job or sudden serious debt, that means you can’t afford the payments. You’ll need evidence of this too.

Unfair terms

Some contract terms may be unfair, which means they might not be legally binding. Citizens Advice gives these examples:

  • a minimum term of more than a year
  • the contract renewing automatically without your permission
  • big changes to the facilities you signed up for
  • a big price rise in the middle of your contract

If one of these applies to you, ask the gym to let you leave without a fee.

If the gym says no

  1. Speak to the gym manager, then put your request in writing and explain why it’s unreasonable to keep you in the contract.
  2. Contact the Citizens Advice consumer service for help.

Questions

Can I cancel my gym membership if I’m injured?

A gym should normally let you cancel if a serious injury or illness stops you exercising. You’ll need evidence from a doctor or other medical professional.

Can I leave my gym if I lose my job?

A gym should normally let you cancel if a big change, such as losing your job, means you can’t afford the payments. You’ll need evidence.

Do I have to pay the rest of my contract?

Usually yes, if you just want to leave early. Check your contract, and check whether any terms might be unfair, such as a minimum term longer than a year.
