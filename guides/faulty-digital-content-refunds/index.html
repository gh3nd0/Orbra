Refunds for faulty apps, games and streaming

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Quick answer

If digital content is faulty, you can ask the company to fix or replace it. If it won’t, or takes too long, you can ask for some money back. For App Store purchases, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What counts as digital content

Games, e-books, in-game purchases (including in free apps), film, music and TV streams or downloads, and software you’ve downloaded. Content that doesn’t work on your device, despite claims that it would, can count as faulty.

First, check the problem isn’t your device or internet connection.

Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015

  1. Repair or replacement: write to the company, describe the fault, include screenshots and the purchase date, and ask it to fix the problem within a reasonable time.
  2. Money back: if it won’t fix it, or takes too long, ask for a refund. Unlike faulty goods, you don’t have an automatic right to a full refund. The amount depends on how long you’ve had it, how much you’ve used it and what’s wrong.
  3. Damage to your device: if the content damaged your device, you’re entitled to compensation. Get a repair quote and send it to the company.

Asking Apple for a refund

  1. Sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Choose I’d like to, then Request a refund.
  3. Choose your reason, then the item, and submit.

Apple usually replies within one to two days.

If you also want to stop future payments, cancel the subscription as well. See our guide to cancelling subscriptions on iPhone.

If the company won’t help

If you paid by debit or credit card, ask your bank about a chargeback. You can also ask whether the company uses an alternative dispute resolution (ADR) scheme, or contact the Citizens Advice consumer service.

Questions

Can I get a full refund for a faulty app or game?

Not automatically. You can ask for it to be fixed or replaced, and if that doesn’t happen, ask for some money back. How much depends on how long you’ve had it, how much you’ve used it and what’s wrong.

How do I ask Apple for a refund?

Sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com, choose I’d like to, then Request a refund, pick your reason and the item, and submit.

What if a download damaged my phone or computer?

You’re entitled to compensation under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Get a repair quote and send it to the company with your evidence.

Sources

  2. Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 1 Chapter 3 (digital content), legislation.gov.uk.
  3. Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple, Apple Support (UK).
