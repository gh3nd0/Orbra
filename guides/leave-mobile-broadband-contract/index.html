How to leave a mobile or broadband contract

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quick answer

For mobile, text PAC to 65075 to keep your number, or STAC to 75075 if you don’t need it, then give the code to your new network. For broadband, just contact your new provider: they arrange the switch for you.

Check your contract first

If you’re still in your minimum term, you may have to pay an early termination charge. The text-to-switch reply tells you if there is one, so you can check before you decide.

Switching mobile network

  1. To keep your number, text PAC to 65075. If you don’t need your number, text STAC to 75075.
  2. Your network must reply within one minute with your code and any early termination charges.
  3. Give the code to your new network. It’s valid for 30 days.

Your new network must complete the switch within one working day, and your old network can’t charge you for any notice period after the switch date.

Switching broadband or landline

You don’t need to contact your old provider. Your new provider arranges the switch using One Touch Switch, and your old service is stopped once the new one is working.

  • Any loss of service during the switch shouldn’t last more than one working day.
  • You won’t pay your old provider for a notice period after the switch date.

Questions

What’s the difference between a PAC and a STAC?

A PAC lets you keep your phone number when you switch. A STAC is for when you don’t need to keep it. Text PAC to 65075 or STAC to 75075.

Do I still pay my old network’s notice period?

No. When you switch using a PAC or STAC, your old provider can’t charge you for any notice period after the switching date.

Do I need to call my old broadband provider to leave?

No. With One Touch Switch, your new provider arranges the switch for you.
