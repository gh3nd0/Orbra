New UK subscription rules from January 2027

Last updated: [fill in when checked]

Quick answer

From 1 January 2027, subscriptions you start must give you clear information up front, remind you before trials end and long contracts renew, let you cancel easily, and give you 14 days to cancel after a trial converts or a long contract renews.

What’s changing

The rules come from the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. The detailed regulations, published in draft, are due to start on 1 January 2027.

  • Clearer information before you sign up.
  • Reminders in writing, including before a free or discounted trial ends and before a contract of 12 months or more renews.
  • Easy cancellation without unnecessary hurdles. If you signed up online, you must be able to cancel online. Companies can make you an offer to stay, but it mustn’t drag the process out.
  • A new 14-day cooling-off period after a trial turns into a paid subscription, and after a contract of 12 months or more renews.

Refunds in the renewal cooling-off period

If you cancel in this period, you’ll get a refund, minus a fair amount for any service you’ve used. Refunds must be paid within 14 days of cancelling.

Who it applies to

The rules cover subscriptions you take out on or after 1 January 2027. Under the government’s draft guidance, existing subscriptions aren’t covered, even when they renew.

Some charity memberships for cultural and heritage organisations are excluded.

Until then

You still have the current 14-day cooling-off period when you sign up online or by phone, and Ofcom’s rules on phone and broadband price rises.

Questions

When do the new subscription rules start?

1 January 2027. They apply to subscriptions taken out on or after that date.

Do the new rules cover my existing subscriptions?

Under the government’s draft guidance, no. They apply to new subscriptions taken out on or after 1 January 2027, even if your existing ones renew after that.

What is the renewal cooling-off period?

A new 14-day period to cancel after a free or discounted trial turns into a paid subscription, or after a contract of 12 months or more renews.
