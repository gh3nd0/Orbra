How to cancel Spotify Premium

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quick answer

Sign in to your Spotify account page in a browser, go to Manage your plan and select Cancel subscription. Premium carries on until your next billing date, then your account moves to the free plan.

Cancel from your account page

  1. Sign in to your Spotify account page in a web browser.
  2. Go to Manage your plan.
  3. Select Cancel subscription and follow the steps.

What happens next

  • Premium stays until your next billing date. After that, your account switches to the free plan, with ads.
  • You keep your playlists and saved music.
  • If you cancel during a free trial, you move to the free plan straight away, and you can’t restart that trial.

Family and Duo plans

Only the plan manager can cancel a Family or Duo plan. If you’re a member, following the steps above just removes you from the plan. Ask the plan manager to cancel it.

If you pay through a partner

If you can’t see the option to change or cancel your plan, you probably get Premium through a partner, such as your mobile network or broadband provider. Check the Payment section under Manage your plan, and contact the partner to cancel.

Questions

Will I lose my playlists if I cancel Premium?

No. Your account moves to the free plan, and you keep your playlists and saved music.

When does Premium actually stop?

On your next billing date. The exception is a free trial: cancelling during a trial moves you to the free plan immediately.

I’m on a Family plan. How do I cancel?

Only the plan manager can cancel a Family or Duo plan. Members can only remove themselves from it.
