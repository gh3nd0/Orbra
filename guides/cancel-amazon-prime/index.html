How to cancel Amazon Prime and get a refund

Last updated: [fill in when checked]

Quick answer

Go to Your Prime Membership on amazon.co.uk, select Manage, then Update, Cancel and More, and follow the steps. If you haven’t used any Prime benefits, you can get a full refund.

End your Prime membership

  1. Sign in to amazon.co.uk and go to Your Prime Membership.
  2. Select Manage.
  3. Select Update, Cancel and More.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to end your membership or free trial.

Cancelling a free trial this way stops it turning into a paid membership.

Can you get a refund?

Amazon’s help page sets out three cases for paid members:

  • Full refund if you haven’t placed an order using Prime benefits.
  • Possible partial refund if you’ve only used Prime delivery.
  • No refund if you’ve used Prime Video, Prime Music or Prime Gaming.

Signed up in the Android app?

If you joined Prime through the Amazon shopping app on Android, you have to manage it through your Google Play subscriptions, not on the Amazon website.

Prime for students and 18 to 24-year-olds

If you cancel during the six-month trial and rejoin later, you won’t get the trial again. You’ll only be offered the discounted paid membership.

Questions

Will I get my money back if I cancel Prime?

If you’re a paid member and haven’t ordered using Prime benefits, you can get a full refund. If you’ve only used Prime delivery, you may get a partial refund. If you’ve watched Prime Video or used Prime Music or Prime Gaming, you won’t get a refund.

How do I stop a Prime free trial from charging me?

End it from Your Prime Membership, using Manage and then Update, Cancel and More. That stops it turning into a paid membership.

Why can’t I cancel Prime on the Amazon website?

If you signed up in the Amazon app on an Android phone, Prime is billed through Google Play. Cancel it in your Google Play subscriptions.
