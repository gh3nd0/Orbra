How to cancel a subscription on iPhone or iPad

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quick answer

Open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose the subscription and tap Cancel Subscription. If it isn’t listed, it’s billed by another company, so cancel with them.

Cancel in Settings

This works for anything you pay for through your Apple Account, including subscriptions you started inside an app from the App Store.

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the subscription you want to stop.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You may need to scroll down to see it.

The steps are the same on an iPad.

If the subscription isn’t there

Only subscriptions billed through Apple show up in Settings. If you signed up on a company’s website, or the charge comes through your mobile network, it won’t be listed.

  • Look at your bank or card statement to see which company is taking the money.
  • Cancel with that company, usually in your account settings on their website.
  • If it’s added to your phone bill, contact your mobile network.

Cancelling a free trial

Apple says to cancel a trial at least 24 hours before it ends. Leave it later and you may be charged for the first paid period.

Questions

Why can’t I see my subscription in the list?

It probably isn’t billed through Apple. Check your bank or card statement to see which company is taking the payment, then cancel with that company directly.

How can I tell it’s cancelled?

Open the subscription again. If there’s no Cancel Subscription button, or you see an expiry message in red, it has already been cancelled.

When should I cancel a free trial?

Apple says to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don’t want it to turn into a paid subscription.
