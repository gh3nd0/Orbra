How to cancel Apple TV (formerly Apple TV+)

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Quick answer

Go to tv.apple.com, sign in, select your account icon, then Settings. Under Subscriptions select Manage, then Cancel Subscription. You can also cancel in Settings on your iPhone.

Cancel on the web

  1. Go to tv.apple.com.
  2. Sign in at the top of the page if you need to.
  3. Select your account icon at the top of the page.
  4. Select Settings.
  5. Under Subscriptions, select Manage.
  6. Select Cancel Subscription.

Cancel on an iPhone or iPad

Apple TV is billed like any other Apple subscription, so you can cancel it in Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. See our step-by-step guide to cancelling a subscription on iPhone or iPad.

If you pay through Google Play or Amazon

  • Google Play: cancel in the Apple TV app on your Android device, or at play.google.com.
  • Amazon: you can only cancel through Amazon.

Questions

Is Apple TV the same as Apple TV+?

Yes. Apple’s streaming subscription used to be called Apple TV+ and is now called Apple TV. You cancel it the same way.

Can I cancel Apple TV without an iPhone?

Yes. Go to tv.apple.com in any web browser, sign in, and cancel from Settings under Subscriptions.

I pay for Apple TV through Amazon. How do I cancel?

Subscriptions billed by Amazon can only be cancelled through Amazon.
